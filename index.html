FAQ

Common questions

Can you take over an existing Drupal site someone else built?

Yes, that's a large part of my work. I start by auditing the codebase, modules, hosting and deployment setup, then give you a clear picture of what's healthy, what's risky and what to fix first.

How do you make sure an upgrade doesn't break my site?

Upgrades are planned in stages and covered by PHPUnit (Kernel/Functional) and Playwright end-to-end tests on your critical user journeys, so regressions are caught before anything reaches production.

Can you add AI features to my Drupal site?

Yes. As a core contributor to Drupal AI, I build LLM-powered content tools, chatbots, semantic search and MCP-based workflows that fit into your existing Drupal setup.

Do you work hourly or fixed-price?

Both. I've delivered hourly and fixed-price contracts on Upwork. After a short call about your project, I'll suggest whichever fits the scope best.
